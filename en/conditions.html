Terms of Use

Updated: October 22, 2026

1. Purpose

Stopododo is an Android app offering a sleep timer, screen-dimming features and end-of-timer actions such as muting sound, attempting to pause playback and, when enabled by the user, locking the screen.

2. Compatibility

Some features depend on the Android version, device manufacturer, media player and system restrictions. Video pausing, precise volume control or extending the filter to system areas cannot be guaranteed on every device.

3. Stopododo Premium

Stopododo Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play. It unlocks timers up to 240 minutes and advanced features including advanced volume control, blue-light filter and advanced full-screen filter. It is not a subscription.

The transaction is processed by Google Play. Applicable payment, refund and restoration conditions are those of Google Play.

4. Optional permissions

Some features require specific Android permissions. They are requested only when the corresponding feature is used. If a permission is refused or disabled, that feature may be unavailable.

5. Screen locking

If you enable device administration for automatic locking, Android may apply its own security rules at the next unlock, including requiring a PIN or password depending on the device and configuration.

6. Accessibility Service

Optional AccessibilityService use is described on the dedicated page and in the Privacy Policy.

7. Changes

Features and compatibility may evolve between versions. Material changes regarding paid features or privacy will be presented appropriately.
