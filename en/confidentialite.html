Privacy Policy

Updated: October 22, 2026

1. About Stopododo

Stopododo is an Android sleep timer and screen-dimming application. For privacy or data questions, contact gogostudio87@gmail.com.

2. Data processed by the app

Stopododo does not create user accounts and does not operate its own server to store your personal data. App settings such as timer duration, playback preferences, filter levels, language and some cached Premium states are stored locally on your device.

Stopododo includes no ad network, audience analytics tool or advertising tracking SDK.

3. Stopododo Premium purchase

Stopododo Premium is offered as a one-time purchase through Google Play. Purchase, payment, verification and restoration are handled by Google Play and the Google Play Billing SDK. Stopododo does not receive your card number or full banking details. The app may receive technical information from Google Play to determine whether Premium is owned and enable the corresponding features.

Google’s processing through Google Play is governed by Google’s applicable privacy rules and terms.

4. Android permissions

Depending on the features you use, Stopododo may request or use certain Android permissions:

  • Display over other apps: applies the dimming or blue-light filter over apps you use.
  • Notifications and foreground service: show the status of an active timer or filter and related controls.
  • Device administration: optional, used only if you choose automatic screen locking at the end of the timer.
  • Audio settings: used for volume and mute functions requested by the user.
  • Accessibility Service: optional, used only for the advanced full-screen filter. See the dedicated page.

5. Accessibility Service

Stopododo’s Accessibility Service is used only to extend the dimming filter to certain system areas when the device allows it. It is configured with canRetrieveWindowContent=false. Stopododo does not use it to read, record or analyse your screen content, typing, messages or actions in other apps.

Activation is optional and requires an explicit action in Android settings. You can disable it at any time.

6. Locally stored data

Preferences and states needed for operation are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. They generally remain until changed, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Stopododo does not send these preferences to a server operated by the publisher.

7. Data sharing

Stopododo does not sell your data and does not share it with advertisers. Technical information related to a Premium purchase may be processed by Google Play to execute, verify and restore the purchase.

8. Security

Stopododo does not allow clear-text network traffic in its Android configuration. Core features that do not require Google Play are designed to work locally on the device. No system can guarantee zero risk.

9. Children

Stopododo is not specifically designed for children and does not include social or advertising features targeted at children.

10. Website

This static website contains no advertising, audience analytics or advertising cookies added by Stopododo. The hosting provider may process technical information strictly necessary to provide its service.

11. Your requests

Because Stopododo has no user account and no personal database operated by the publisher, there is normally no remote profile to delete. For any privacy-related request, write to gogostudio87@gmail.com.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated if Stopododo’s features or associated processing change. The date above will be updated accordingly.
