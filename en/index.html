FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it work on Android TV?+

No. Stopododo is designed for compatible Android phones and tablets, not Android TV devices.

Will it pause every video?+

Not necessarily. The app can try to pause when the video player supports it. Muting media volume is also available.

Is Premium a subscription?+

No. Premium is a €2.99 one-time purchase through Google Play.

Do I need an account?+

No Stopododo account is required. Google Play handles Premium purchases.
