Frequently asked questions

Is Stopododo free?

Yes. The free version includes a timer up to 15 minutes. Premium is a one-time €2.99 purchase, not a subscription.

Can it stop videos automatically?

It can mute audio and try to pause compatible video players. Screen locking is also available with your permission.

Do I need an account?

No Stopododo account is needed. Settings remain on your device; Google Play processes Premium purchases.
